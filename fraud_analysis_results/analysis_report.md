# Financial Fraud & Risk Analytics

- Transactions analysed: 284,807
- Labelled fraud transactions: 492
- Other transactions: 284,315
- Labelled fraud rate: 0.1727%

## Limitations
The amount thresholds are illustrative, not production fraud rules. Results are exploratory and have not been validated on a separate test set. Class 0 means not labelled as fraud in this dataset, not guaranteed legitimate.
